Rain and sleet checkpoint re-evaluation with distance-stratified official K-Radar AP@IoU 0.3.

Distance is the radar-center norm sqrt(x^2+y^2+z^2). GT and detections are filtered independently. Bins are [0,30), [30,60), [60,90), and [90,120) metres. Every value is the mean over epochs 5-24 inclusive. TD = target-trained AP - source-trained AP.
